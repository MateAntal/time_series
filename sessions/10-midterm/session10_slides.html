    Three things reliably cause trouble every year, and they are worth checking first: the difference between seasonality and a cycle (Session 3), why the seasonal period must be even or odd for different moving averages (Session 6), and the difference between a fitted value and a forecast (Session 8). If you are solid on those three, the rest tends to follow.
