# Group assignments

Two group assignments, 20 % of the course grade between them. Both receive detailed written feedback.

## 1 — Classical decomposition (released Session 7, due Sunday 25 October 2026)

[`assignment-1-classical-decomposition/`](assignment-1-classical-decomposition/)

Reproduce `classical_decomposition()` exactly on Victorian liquor retailing turnover, for both the
additive and the multiplicative scheme, verify with `all.equal()`, redraw the output in ggplot
without `autoplot()`, and judge the two decompositions against the two criteria from Session 7.

Assignment 1 runs for four weeks and is due **Sunday 25 October 2026, 23:59** — after the midterm,
so that exam revision and a from-scratch implementation do not compete for the same week.

## 2 — The Efficient Market Hypothesis (released Session 17, due Sunday 6 December 2026)

Released on **Monday 23 November 2026**. The assignment file and the data it needs appear in this
folder on that date.

The EMH as a random walk. Compare the naïve method against simple exponential smoothing and damped
Holt, with error metrics computed both via `accuracy()` and by hand, then full cross-validation.
